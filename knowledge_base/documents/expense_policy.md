# Asteria Systems — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy ID:** EXP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Policy owner:** Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines which business expenses may be reimbursed and how employees must submit reimbursement requests.

Only expenses with a legitimate business purpose are eligible for reimbursement.

## 2. Submission Deadline

Employees must submit reimbursement requests within **10 calendar days** after the expense occurs.

For expenses related to business travel, the 10-day period begins on the employee's return date.

Requests submitted after the deadline may require additional review by Finance.

## 3. Receipt Requirements

A receipt is required for any individual expense of **R$ 50 or more**.

For expenses below R$ 50, a receipt is recommended but is not mandatory.

Employees must not split a single expense into multiple smaller expenses in order to avoid the receipt requirement.

## 4. Eligible Expenses

Examples of potentially reimbursable business expenses include:

- approved business travel;
- business meals;
- local transportation for business purposes;
- approved office supplies;
- approved professional event registration.

The expense must comply with any other policy that applies to the purchase.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are not reimbursable:

- alcoholic beverages;
- traffic or parking fines;
- personal entertainment;
- personal clothing;
- personal subscriptions;
- personal gifts;
- expenses without a legitimate business purpose.

## 6. Approval

Reimbursement requests require approval from the employee's direct manager.

Expenses above **R$ 2,000** also require review by Finance.

Approval of a reimbursement request does not modify any spending limit established by another company policy.

The Employee Policy Assistant may explain the approval process but cannot approve reimbursement requests.

## 7. Processing Time

Finance aims to process approved reimbursement requests within **7 business days** after receiving all required documentation.

The 7-business-day period is a processing target and is **not a guaranteed payment date**.

## 8. Accuracy of Claims

Employees are responsible for providing accurate information and documentation.

Submitting false, duplicated, or intentionally misleading expenses may result in additional review under company procedures.
