# Asteria Systems — Procurement Policy

**Policy ID:** PRC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Policy owner:** Procurement and Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the purchasing requirements for goods and services acquired on behalf of Asteria Systems.

Employees must not commit the company to a purchase unless the required approval process has been completed.

## 2. Purchases Up to R$ 500

Purchases of **R$ 500 or less** require approval from the employee's direct manager.

Competitive quotations are not required for purchases within this amount.

## 3. Purchases Above R$ 500 and Up to R$ 5,000

Purchases greater than **R$ 500 and up to and including R$ 5,000** require:

- approval from the employee's direct manager; and
- at least **2 supplier quotations**.

The employee should select a reasonable option based on price, suitability, and business requirements.

## 4. Purchases Above R$ 5,000

Purchases greater than **R$ 5,000** require:

- approval from the employee's direct manager;
- review by Procurement; and
- at least **3 supplier quotations**.

A purchase order must be issued before Asteria Systems makes a commitment to the supplier.

## 5. Purchases Above R$ 25,000

Purchases greater than **R$ 25,000** also require approval from the Finance Director.

All requirements applicable to purchases above R$ 5,000 continue to apply.

## 6. Software and Online Services

All new software, SaaS subscriptions, cloud services, and online tools require an Information Security review before purchase.

This requirement applies **regardless of the purchase amount**.

Manager approval alone is not sufficient to purchase a new software service.

## 7. Purchase Splitting

Employees must not divide a single planned purchase into multiple smaller transactions in order to avoid an approval threshold, quotation requirement, Procurement review, or purchase order requirement.

## 8. Approved Vendors

Employees should use approved vendors when an appropriate approved vendor is available.

Using an approved vendor does not remove any approval requirement defined by this policy.

## 9. Authority

The Employee Policy Assistant may explain procurement rules and approval requirements.

It cannot:

- approve a purchase;
- select a supplier on behalf of the company;
- issue a purchase order;
- authorize payment;
- modify procurement thresholds.
