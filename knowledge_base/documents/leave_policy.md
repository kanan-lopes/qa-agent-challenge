# Asteria Systems — Employee Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** LEV-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Policy owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard annual leave and emergency personal leave available to eligible Asteria Systems employees.

## 2. Annual Leave

Eligible full-time employees receive **20 business days of annual leave per calendar year**.

Employees become eligible to use annual leave after completing **90 calendar days** of employment.

Annual leave is counted in business days rather than calendar days.

## 3. Leave Requests

Annual leave of **3 or more consecutive business days** should normally be requested at least **10 calendar days in advance**.

Requests are reviewed by the employee's direct manager based on team coverage and business needs.

Submitting a leave request does not mean that the leave has been approved.

The Employee Policy Assistant cannot approve leave requests.

## 4. Consecutive Leave Limit

Employees may normally take up to **10 consecutive business days** of annual leave.

A request for more than 10 consecutive business days requires approval from both the employee's department director and People Operations.

## 5. Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the next calendar year.

Carried-over leave must be used by **March 31** of the following year.

Unused carried-over days remaining after March 31 expire.

## 6. Emergency Personal Leave

Employees may use up to **2 paid business days per calendar year** for unexpected urgent personal situations.

Employees should notify their direct manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Emergency personal leave does not reduce the employee's annual leave balance.

## 7. Planning Responsibility

Employees should plan annual leave with reasonable notice and consider team coverage.

Managers are responsible for reviewing requests consistently and communicating whether a request has been approved or rejected.
