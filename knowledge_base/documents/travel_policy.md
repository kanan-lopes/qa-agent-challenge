# Asteria Systems — Business Travel Policy

**Policy ID:** TRV-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Policy owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for employees traveling on behalf of Asteria Systems.

Business travel must have a clear work-related purpose and must be approved before any non-refundable booking is made.

## 2. Travel Approval

All business travel requires approval from the employee's direct manager before booking.

International travel also requires approval from the employee's department director.

The Employee Policy Assistant may explain these requirements but cannot approve or authorize travel.

## 3. Air Travel

Economy class is required for domestic flights.

Economy class is also required for international flights shorter than 8 hours.

Premium economy may be booked for an international flight with a scheduled duration of 8 hours or more, provided that the employee receives approval from the department director before booking.

Business class and first class are not reimbursable under this policy.

## 4. Accommodation

For domestic business travel, hotel expenses are reimbursable up to **R$ 450 per night**.

For international business travel, hotel expenses are reimbursable up to **R$ 800 per night**.

Amounts above these limits require written approval from the department director before the hotel is booked.

Personal expenses charged by the hotel, such as minibar purchases, movies, spa services, or leisure activities, are not reimbursable.

## 5. Meals

For domestic business travel, employees may claim up to **R$ 120 per day** for meals.

For international business travel, employees may claim up to **R$ 200 per day** for meals.

These are maximum daily reimbursement limits, not automatic allowances. Employees may only claim expenses actually incurred.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

## 6. Local Transportation

Reasonable taxi, rideshare, public transportation, and rental vehicle expenses may be reimbursed when they are necessary for business travel.

Transportation expenses must have a clear business purpose.

## 7. Travel Expense Claims

Travel-related expenses must also follow the requirements defined in the Asteria Systems Expense Reimbursement Policy.

Employees must provide receipts whenever required by that policy.

## 8. Employee Responsibility

Employees are responsible for choosing reasonable travel options and complying with the limits defined in this policy.

An approved business trip does not automatically mean that every expense incurred during the trip is reimbursable.
