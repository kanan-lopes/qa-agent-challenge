# Asteria Systems — Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** RMT-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Policy owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard remote work arrangements available to eligible employees of Asteria Systems.

## 2. Standard Hybrid Schedule

Eligible hybrid employees may work remotely for up to **2 days per week**.

Employees are expected to work from an Asteria Systems office for at least **3 days per week**.

Unused remote work days cannot be carried over to another week.

A recurring remote work schedule must be agreed with the employee's direct manager.

## 3. Temporary Exceptions

A temporary remote work exception may allow an eligible employee to work remotely for up to **10 consecutive business days**.

Temporary exceptions require approval from both:

- the employee's direct manager; and
- People Operations.

An exception is not automatically granted because an employee requests it.

## 4. Work Location

Standard remote work must be performed from within Brazil.

Working remotely from another country requires prior written approval from People Operations and Legal.

Employees must not assume that an international remote work request has been approved until the required approval has been provided.

## 5. Availability

Remote employees must remain reasonably reachable during their normal working hours.

All remote employees must be available for meetings and communication during the company's core collaboration period from **10:00 to 16:00 Brasília time**, unless an approved work arrangement states otherwise.

## 6. Information Security

Employees working remotely must follow Asteria Systems information security requirements.

Company confidential information must not be intentionally shared with unauthorized people.

Employees must use company-approved systems when accessing confidential company information.

## 7. Business Needs

Managers may require an employee to attend the office on a normally scheduled remote work day when there is a legitimate business need, such as an important in-person meeting, workshop, or operational requirement.

## 8. Authority

The Employee Policy Assistant may explain the remote work policy.

It cannot approve remote work schedules, temporary exceptions, or international remote work requests.
